TalentDikhao – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

TalentDikhao respects user privacy and is committed to protecting user data. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use the TalentDikhao mobile application.

Information We Collect

We may collect user-provided information such as profile details, posts, reels, comments, messages, photos, videos, audio, and basic app usage data to provide core social and talent discovery features. With your permission, the app may access your device camera, microphone, and photo gallery to allow you to capture and upload content.

How We Use Information

  • To provide social networking, talent showcase, and collaboration features
  • To enable authentication, cloud storage, and real-time interactions
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To maintain community safety, moderate content, and prevent misuse

Third-Party Services

TalentDikhao uses trusted cloud infrastructure services (such as Google Firebase) for authentication, database storage, and media hosting. These services process data strictly to operate the app in accordance with their security standards.

Data Sharing

TalentDikhao does not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties. Information is shared only as necessary to provide core app functionality or comply with legal obligations.

User Control & Data Deletion

Users can edit their profile details or request deletion of their account and associated data at any time through the app settings or by contacting us via email.

Child & Community Safety

TalentDikhao is built to be safe and welcoming for creators of all ages. Any abusive, harmful, or inappropriate content is strictly prohibited and can be reported for removal.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and administrative security measures to protect user information against unauthorized access or loss.
